Need to move files from your PC to your phone? Meet Line: share files between Windows and Android, with no account or cloud storage. Nearby connects devices on the same local network. Internet mode needs a reachable public address and compatible network access. On your PC, choose Nearby. Click Choose files and select your files. Then click Create sharing room. Your device hosts the room. Share the full invitation and the temporary eight-digit code. On the other device, open Receive. Paste the invitation and enter the code. Then tap Join room. Compare the security codes on both screens. Then tap Accept and receive. Keep Line running and watch the progress on both devices. Your files travel encrypted. On Android, save a copy to a folder on your phone. Line. Your files. No detours.
