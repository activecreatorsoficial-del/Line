# Line - English short video production brief

## Deliverable

Create a 70-second vertical product introduction and practical walkthrough. Target: YouTube Shorts, Instagram Reels and TikTok. Format: 1080 x 1920, 9:16, 30 fps, H.264 MP4 with AAC audio. Narration, subtitles, overlays and end card are in English. Main demonstration: Windows sender to Android receiver in Nearby mode. The audience should understand what Line does and be able to repeat the transfer.

An optional 30-second Nearby-only cut is included in 05-optional-30s.md. Produce the 70-second version unless the user specifically chooses the shorter edit. Do not exceed 80 seconds.

## Product facts to preserve

- Line shares selected files between Windows and Android devices. It is for sending a copy, not permanent remote disk access or a cloud drive.
- No account is required. In the demonstrated default flow, files are not uploaded to central storage.
- The device creating the room is its temporary host. Share the FULL invitation plus the separate eight-digit temporary access code. A short room name alone is insufficient.
- Nearby is for devices on the same local network, such as the same Wi-Fi or hotspot. An Ethernet PC and Wi-Fi phone can also be on the same local network.
- Internet mode first tries compatible UPnP access with reachable public IPv4, then native public IPv6 when IPv4 discovery fails. IPv6 rooms need IPv6 on both devices. CGNAT blocks the IPv4 path; IPv6 incoming filtering and other network restrictions can still prevent access. This is not guaranteed on every home or mobile connection. No public relay or coordinator is added. Outside-network IPv6 access has not been verified.
- Compare the security codes shown on both screens before the receiver accepts. These are not the same as the eight-digit room access code.
- Keep Line running and the network connected until completion. Android has an active sharing notification, so do not claim its screen must remain on.
- Received Android files first go into Line's private storage. Use Save to a folder on your phone to export a copy accessible to other apps.
- The current release is a preview. Do not invent Play Store/Microsoft Store availability, a public download site, prices, speed records or unlimited file sizes.

## Narration

Use 02-voiceover-70s.txt. Aim for a clear, warm, natural English delivery with space for the tutorial clicks and the final brand beat. Pronounce Line as the English word "line". Avoid an exaggerated sales voice. Keep the product purpose and instructions dominant.

The supplied SRT is a timing guide. Align the cuts and captions to the finished voiceover. The final export should stay around 70 seconds, with an absolute maximum of 80 seconds. Do not simply speed up unreadable instructions to fit.

## Visual direction

- Use the actual monochrome Line look: warm charcoal #201F1D, off-white #F7F7F4, gray #65625E and the supplied Inter font.
- Line's app icon is TWO OPPOSING ARROWS, using assets/line-icon.png. Do not replace it with a green icon or the Active Creators flame.
- Active Creators artwork can appear as a small secondary credit or end-card detail. It must not be mistaken for the Line app icon.
- Screenshots in references/ are genuine interface reference images. Keep their proportions, wording, logo and layout. No invented settings, buttons, drag-and-drop flows or QR-code joining.
- Prefer authentic screen recordings for actions and transfer states. Missing states should be covered with clearly editorial instruction cards beside reference screenshots, not fake app controls.
- If transfer progress is schematic, label it "Illustrative demo" and do not show fabricated MB/s values, large-file timings or benchmark claims. A time-compressed real transfer must be visibly an edit, not a speed claim.
- Use an establishing device view briefly, then crop and zoom into the control relevant to the narration. Avoid an entire desktop squeezed into a vertical frame with unreadable labels.
- Use clean cuts, subtle cursor/touch highlights, short push-ins and restrained file-movement graphics. Effects support the explanation. No flashing, excessive shake or transitions that cover a button during its instruction.
- Make subtitles high contrast, approximately 48-60 px at 1080-wide output, maximum two lines. Keep key captions and controls away from platform overlays: generous top margin, right-side action-button clearance, and roughly the bottom 300-350 px clear of essential details.
- Keep the Internet restriction readable during 11-21 seconds. Show "Internet: reachable public IPv4 or IPv6. Network restrictions apply." Both devices need IPv6 for an IPv6 room. Do not frame Internet mode as universal access from anywhere.

## Sound

Use a restrained instrumental electronic bed, no vocals, with a calm pulse around 95-110 BPM. Use only audio licensed for the intended publication. Keep narration clearly above the music and duck the bed underneath speech. Add soft clicks for actual actions, one subtle connection sound and a restrained completion chime. No loud whooshes or fake system notifications.
